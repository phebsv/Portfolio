STRIDE

Safe Travel Routing and Incident Detection Engine

Overview

A pedestrian-safety routing application focused on Cebu City that evaluates walking routes using incident records, community reports, and relevant risk factors.

Role

Project Leader / Backend Developer

Contributions

  • Implemented map display and current-location functionality.
  • Worked on destination search and walking-route retrieval.
  • Developed the route risk-scoring algorithm.
  • Integrated CCPO incident data and community-reported incidents into risk evaluation.
  • Considered factors such as incident severity, recency, density, proximity, environmental conditions, distance, and travel time.
  • Managed code revisions and collaborated through GitHub.

Technologies

  • NativePHP
  • Laravel / PHP
  • PostgreSQL
  • OpenStreetMap
  • GitHub
Disclaimer

Risk scores are estimates, not guarantees of safety. The absence of reports does not mean a location is safe.
